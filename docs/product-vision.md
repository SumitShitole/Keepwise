# Product vision

Keepwise is a personal ownership assistant. It helps people in India track what they own, when they bought it, and when warranty, returns, maintenance, or insurance need attention.

Users should not have to remember dates or type every purchase by hand. When they share a receipt, PDF, or order text, Keepwise extracts a **purchase candidate**, asks them to confirm, then creates the asset and schedules reminders.

Keepwise does not read private SMS or WhatsApp inboxes. Capture is always user-initiated (upload, paste, later email forwarding / share sheet).

The important KPI is **Automatic Capture Rate**: confirmed auto-created assets divided by all assets created.
