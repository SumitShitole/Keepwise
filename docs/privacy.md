# Privacy

Keepwise stores email, purchase metadata, and documents the user uploads. Automatic detection only runs on content the user submits.

**Not requested:** Android `READ_SMS` / `RECEIVE_SMS`, WhatsApp inbox access, full mailbox download.

Users can disable receipt scanning and AI processing, delete imported candidates, and export their data. Disconnecting Gmail/Outlook is Phase 2.

When AI is enabled, extracted text (not unnecessary raw dumps) may be sent to the configured OCR/LLM provider. Data is not used to train Keepwise models. Legal review is required for DPDP and third-party processing (including Firebase Auth). This is not legal advice.
